# Fix Wi-Fi Connection Problems

**Labels:** `wifi` `wireless` `network` `internet`
**Category:** Network & VPN

---

## Who this is for

You can't join the office Wi-Fi, or it's connected but painfully slow.

---

## Try this first

1. **Toggle Wi-Fi off, wait 10 seconds, toggle it on.**
2. **Forget the network and rejoin it.** This clears a stale saved password, which is the most common cause.
   * Windows: Settings → Network & Internet → Wi-Fi → Manage known networks → select the network → Forget
   * Mac: System Settings → Wi-Fi → Details → Forget This Network
3. **Check you're on the right network.** `Nimbus-Corp` is for staff. `Nimbus-Guest` is slow and blocks internal systems on purpose.

---

## It won't connect at all

1. **Is airplane mode on?** Check the obvious one first.
2. **Is the physical Wi-Fi switch off?** Some laptops have one on the side or an `Fn` key combo.
3. **Can other people nearby connect?** If nobody can, it's the access point — tell us, and mention how many people are affected. That changes the priority.
4. **Restart your laptop.** Resets the wireless adapter.

---

## Connected but "no internet"

1. Open a browser and try any public site. Some networks need you to accept a terms page first.
2. Renew your network address:
   * **Windows:** Command Prompt → `ipconfig /release` then `ipconfig /renew`
   * **Mac:** System Settings → Wi-Fi → Details → TCP/IP → Renew DHCP Lease
3. Check whether a VPN is running. A half-connected VPN looks exactly like broken Wi-Fi.

---

## It's connected but very slow

1. **Check the signal strength icon.** One or two bars means move.
2. **Move closer to an access point.** Lifts, kitchens, stairwells and corner meeting rooms are dead zones in most offices.
3. **Switch to 5 GHz** if your laptop shows both bands. It's faster and less crowded, but shorter range.
4. **Plug in an ethernet cable** if you're doing something important. Always faster than Wi-Fi.
5. **Close cloud backup and sync apps.** They quietly eat the whole connection.

---

## Only one specific room is bad

Note the exact room name or number. Repeated reports from one location get us to fix the access point permanently rather than resetting your laptop again.

---

## Still stuck?

👉 Raise a **Wi-Fi Issue** request in the portal.

Tell us:
* Which floor / room you're in
* Which network name you're trying to join
* Whether other people around you have the same problem
* Whether it's "won't connect" or "connected but slow"

**Target response:** 4 hours during business hours. If a whole floor is down, say so — that becomes highest priority.
