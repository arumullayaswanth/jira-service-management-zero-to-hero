# My Laptop Is Slow

**Labels:** `laptop` `slow` `performance` `hardware`
**Category:** Laptop & Hardware

---

## Who this is for

Your laptop takes forever to start, apps freeze, or everything feels like wading through mud.

---

## Try this first

1. **Restart it.** Not sleep, not close-the-lid — a full restart. If your laptop has been awake for three weeks, this alone usually fixes it.
2. **Close browser tabs.** Forty tabs will eat all your memory. Bookmark them instead.
3. **Check for a pending update.** A half-installed update runs background work that slows everything down. Let it finish.

---

## Find out what's actually eating your machine

**Windows:**
1. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` to open Task Manager
2. Click the **CPU** column to sort by it, then the **Memory** column
3. Note the top 3 offenders

**Mac:**
1. Open **Activity Monitor** (`Cmd + Space`, type "Activity Monitor")
2. Sort by **% CPU**, then by **Memory**
3. Note the top 3

📸 **Screenshot that list and attach it to your ticket.** It tells us in five seconds what would otherwise take a 20-minute call.

---

## Free up disk space

A drive that's nearly full makes the whole machine crawl.

1. Check free space:
   * **Windows:** File Explorer → This PC
   * **Mac:** Apple menu → System Settings → General → Storage
2. **Under 10% free is a problem.** Under 5% and things will start failing.
3. Safe things to clear:
   * Downloads folder
   * Empty the Recycle Bin / Trash
   * Old video files and installers
   * Windows: run **Disk Cleanup**
4. Don't delete anything from `Windows`, `Program Files`, `System`, or `Library`.

---

## Stop apps launching at startup

* **Windows:** Task Manager → **Startup apps** tab → right-click anything unnecessary → Disable
* **Mac:** System Settings → General → **Login Items** → select → remove

Disable things like chat apps, music players, and updaters. Leave anything with "security", "antivirus", "endpoint", or the company name in it alone.

---

## Signs it's the hardware, not software

Tell us if you notice any of these — they change our answer completely:

* Loud fan running constantly, and the laptop is hot to touch
* Clicking or grinding noises
* Random shutdowns
* It only runs well when plugged in
* Battery swollen, or the case no longer sits flat 🚨 **stop using it and raise a ticket immediately**
