# How to Request Software

**Labels:** `software` `install` `licence` `application`
**Category:** Software

---

## Who this is for

You need an application installed on your work machine.

---

## Check the self-service catalogue first

Some software is pre-approved and you can install it yourself with no ticket and no waiting:

* Company Portal / Software Centre on Windows
* Self Service app on Mac

If your software is listed there, install it and you're done. No approval needed.

---

## Why some software needs approval

Software that isn't pre-approved goes through a manager approval step. Three reasons:

1. **Cost.** Licences are real money and come out of your department's budget.
2. **Security.** We check the vendor and the data it touches.
3. **Compatibility.** Some tools break other tools.

This is why the request asks for a business justification and your manager's email. Filling those in properly is the difference between approval in an hour and a week of back-and-forth.

---

## How to raise the request

1. Open the Nimbus IT Help Desk portal
2. Choose **Software Installation**
3. Fill in:

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| Exact software name and version | `Figma Desktop, latest` — not just "design tool" |
| Why do you need it? | The work task it enables. One or two sentences. |
| Your manager's email | Their real work address |
| Which machine? | Your asset tag |

4. Submit

---

## Write a justification that gets approved

❌ **Weak:** "I need Figma."

✅ **Strong:** "I need Figma to review design handoffs from the product team. Right now I request PNG exports for every change, which adds about two days to each review cycle."

Say what work it unblocks and what it costs you not to have it. Managers approve those in minutes.

---

## What happens next

```text
You submit
    ▼
Your manager gets an approval email
    ▼
Manager approves or declines
    ▼
If approved: IT checks the licence
    ▼
IT installs it (remotely, or you drop the laptop off)
    ▼
Ticket resolved
```

Typical timeline once approved: **1–2 business days.** Waiting on a manager is the slowest part, so tell them it's coming.

---

## If your software is declined

The ticket will say why. Usual reasons:

* An approved alternative already exists — we'll name it
* No licence budget this quarter
* Security review failed
* It conflicts with something else on your machine

You can reply on the ticket to discuss it. Declined isn't always final.

---

## Please don't install it yourself

Downloading installers from the web puts unlicensed and unvetted software on a company machine. It also means we can't support it when it breaks, and licence audits become our problem. Raise the request — it's genuinely faster than the cleanup.

---

## Questions?

👉 Raise a **Software Installation** request, or **General IT Support** if you're not sure what you need yet.

**Target response:** 8 hours during business hours (this is a Low priority request type — it's a purchase, not an outage).
