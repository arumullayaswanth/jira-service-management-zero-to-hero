# How to Reset Your Password

**Labels:** `password` `login` `locked-out` `mfa`
**Category:** Passwords & Accounts

---

## Who this is for

You forgot your password, or you see "account locked" when you try to sign in.

---

## Try this first

Most lockouts are one of these three things:

1. **Caps Lock is on.** Type your password into a plain text box first to check it.
2. **You changed your password recently** but your phone/email app still has the old one saved, and it keeps retrying and locking you out. Update the saved password on every device.
3. **Wait 15 minutes.** Most account lockouts unlock themselves automatically.

---

## Reset it yourself

1. Go to the company sign-in page.
2. Click **Forgot password?** under the password box.
3. Type your work email address.
4. Check your email for a reset link. It expires in 15 minutes.
5. Click the link and choose a new password.

Your new password needs:

* At least 12 characters
* One capital letter
* One number
* One symbol
* Nothing you have used in your last 5 passwords

---

## If you have no access to your email

You cannot receive the reset link, so use your second factor:

1. Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Choose **Verify another way**
3. Approve the prompt in your authenticator app, or enter the code sent by SMS

---

## Your authenticator app is on a lost or wiped phone

This one needs a human, because we have to verify who you are.

1. Raise a **Password Reset** request in the portal
2. In the description, write: `MFA device lost — need re-enrolment`
3. An agent will call you to verify your identity before resetting

We will never reset MFA from a chat message alone. That is how account takeovers happen.

---

## Still stuck?

👉 Raise a **Password Reset** request in the Nimbus IT Help Desk portal.

Tell us:
* Which system you can't get into
* The exact error message
* Whether you can receive email

**Target response:** 2 hours during business hours.
